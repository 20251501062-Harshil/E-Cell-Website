01 About E-Cell

About E-Cell IIIT Vadodara

E-Cell IIIT Vadodara is the Entrepreneurship Cell of the Indian Institute of Information Technology Vadodara. We help students turn ideas into ventures: we build the skills every founder needs, back promising ideas on their way to becoming startups, connect innovators with the Government of Gujarat's Student Startup & Innovation Policy, and bridge the campus to the startup and industry world.

02 Entrepreneurship

What is entrepreneurship?

It is the work of finding a problem worth solving, building something people genuinely need, and turning it into a venture that can grow.

It is a skill, not a talent. You learn it by doing: talking to users, testing ideas cheaply, building early versions and explaining them clearly. For students it is also one of the best ways to learn, because classroom knowledge turns into something real.

How an idea becomes a startup

  1. 01

    Spot a real problem

    Start with people, not technology. Who is struggling, and what do they do about it today?

  2. 02

    Validate the idea

    Talk to users, test cheaply and look for proof that people actually want it.

  3. 03

    Build an MVP

    Make the smallest version that solves the problem, put it in front of users, learn and improve.

  4. 04

    Pitch with confidence

    Explain the problem, the solution and what you need, clearly, to mentors, judges and investors.

03 What we do

Four wings. One E-Cell.

E-Cell's work is organised into four wings. Each has its own job; together they take a student from a first idea towards a real venture.

Wing 0101 / 04

Skill Development

From curious to capable.

Builds the skills every founder needs. Through sessions, workshops and hands-on activities, this wing helps students learn how to spot real problems, validate ideas, build products, understand the basics of business and pitch with confidence. It takes students from curious to capable.

Wing 0202 / 04

Incubation

From idea to MVP, with mentors.

Supports promising student ideas on their way to becoming startups. This wing guides teams through validation and building an MVP, connects them with mentors and the right resources, and tracks progress, so a good idea gets the structure and momentum it needs to grow.

Wing 0303 / 04

SSIP

Student Startup & Innovation Policy

Connects students with SSIP, the Government of Gujarat's policy that supports student innovation and startups. This wing spreads awareness about SSIP, helps innovators understand the process, and guides them in applying for support for their ideas and prototypes.

Wing 0404 / 04

IRP

Industry Resource Planning

Builds E-Cell's bridge to the startup and industry world. This wing connects us with founders, mentors, companies and partners, bringing in speakers, judges, sponsors and collaborations that create real opportunities for our students.

04 Programmes

Learn it. Pitch it. Hear it.

Workshops

Hands-on. From a problem to a prototype.

Practical sessions run by the Skill Development wing. Students work through the real steps of starting something: spotting problems, validating ideas, building products, understanding the basics of business and pitching.

Pitching events

On stage, in front of the sharks.

Events where student founders present their ideas and face the questions every startup has to answer, led by our flagship, Shark Tank IIITV 2026.

Speaker sessions

Founders, mentors and industry, in conversation.

Through the IRP wing, E-Cell brings in founders, mentors and people from industry as speakers, so students can learn from those who have built companies and ask them questions directly.

Questions every pitch should answer

  1. Is this a real problem, for real people, right now?
  2. What proof do you have that anyone wants it?
  3. Does what you built actually solve it?
  4. If it works, how big can it get?
  5. Why are you the ones to do this?
  6. What do you need, and what will it unlock?
