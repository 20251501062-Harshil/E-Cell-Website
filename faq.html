About E-Cell

What is E-Cell IIIT Vadodara?

E-Cell IIIT Vadodara is the Entrepreneurship Cell of the Indian Institute of Information Technology Vadodara. It helps students turn ideas into ventures: it builds the skills every founder needs, supports promising ideas on their way to becoming startups, connects innovators with SSIP and bridges the campus to the startup and industry world.

What are E-Cell's four wings?
  • Skill Development builds the skills every founder needs through sessions, workshops and hands-on activities.
  • Incubation supports promising student ideas on their way to becoming startups, from validation to an MVP, with mentors and resources.
  • SSIP connects students with the Government of Gujarat's Student Startup & Innovation Policy and guides them in applying for support.
  • IRP (Industry Resource Planning) builds E-Cell's bridge to founders, mentors, companies and partners.

Read about the four wings

What does E-Cell organise?

Workshops, pitching events and speaker sessions, along with industry projects and a network of founders and mentors. Its flagship event is Shark Tank IIITV.

What is SSIP?

SSIP is the Student Startup & Innovation Policy of the Government of Gujarat, which supports student innovation and startups. E-Cell's SSIP wing spreads awareness about it, helps innovators understand the process, and guides them in applying for support for their ideas and prototypes.

Shark Tank IIITV 2026

What is Shark Tank IIITV?

E-Cell's flagship startup pitching competition. Student founders pitch their ideas to a panel of sharks over multiple rounds, leading to the grand finale on campus.

When and where is the grand finale?

Sunday, 1 November 2026, on the IIIT Vadodara campus.

Who can take part?

Students from any college, in teams of 1 to 4.

Is there an entry fee?

No. Entry is free.

How does the competition work?

Teams pitch over multiple rounds, leading to the grand finale on campus.

Does the site store my idea or my deck?

Only on your own device. The idea card, the deck builder and the pitch checklist save what you type in your browser's storage, so it is there when you come back. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I turn off the animations?

Yes. Choose Reduce motion in the menu or the footer. If your phone or computer is set to reduce motion, the site already respects that.
