02 Prepare

Questions every pitch should answer.

Six questions every strong pitch answers. Answer them in order and you have the spine of a pitch.

  1. 01

    Problem

    Is this a real problem, for real people, right now?

    Name the person, the moment it hurts, and what they do about it today.

  2. 02

    Proof

    What proof do you have that anyone wants it?

    Users, pilots, pre-orders, interviews. The smallest real number beats the biggest projection.

  3. 03

    Demo

    Does what you built actually solve it?

    Show the thing. A short demo says more than slides of features.

  4. 04

    Market

    If it works, how big can it get?

    Build it bottom-up: how many people, paying how much, how often.

  5. 05

    Team

    Why are you the ones to do this?

    Lived experience, a rare skill, access to the customer, or visible momentum.

  6. 06

    The ask

    What do you need, and what will it unlock?

    The exact resource, the exact milestone, the exact timeline. A vague ask reads as unready.
